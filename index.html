How it works

A QR code can contain text, website links, Wi-Fi details and other data. Scan an image or use your camera to read its encoded contents. This tool reads standard QR codes; damaged or encrypted content may not be readable.

Privacy

Scanning and generation run in your browser using third-party JavaScript libraries loaded from a CDN. This site does not intentionally upload your QR images or decoded results to its own server. Your browser may request library files from the CDN. Do not scan sensitive QR codes on a device you do not trust.
